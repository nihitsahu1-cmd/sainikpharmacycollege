GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

For official information, please contact your selected institution directly.

Which colleges are part of Sainik Group of Institutions?

The group site lists Sainik PG College, Sainik Law College, Sainik Pharmacy College, Sainik ITI College, Sainik Paramedical & Nursing College, and Sant Ram Lakhan Degree College.

Where can I find eligibility, duration and fees?

These details have not been verified for publication. Please ask the relevant college to confirm current eligibility, course duration and fees.

How do I apply for admission?

Send an enquiry to ask the college about its current application steps and important dates. The website does not provide an online application or admission decision.

Does this enquiry form save or send my details?

No. It validates your entries in this browser and opens a WhatsApp message draft for you to review. The website has no connected backend and does not store or send the form data.
